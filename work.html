Pfizer Extern RAG · Clinical Supply Document Q&A

RAG LlamaIndex Hybrid Retrieval Reranking Evaluation

Built a retrieval-augmented question answering system over clinical supply documentation during Extern's AI-Powered Document Insights & Data Extraction externship with Pfizer.

Pipeline

  • PDF extraction with PyMuPDF, preserving page-level metadata for citation.
  • Sentence-based chunking with overlap using LlamaIndex's SentenceSplitter.
  • Hybrid retrieval: dense embeddings (sentence-transformers) + BM25, fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • Cross-encoder reranking (ms-marco-MiniLM) on top of hybrid candidates.
  • Grounded generation with Google Gemini, with inline page citations for verifiability.

Evaluation

  • Labeled query set with expected source pages; measured retrieval accuracy @ top-3.
  • 86% top-3 retrieval accuracy; known failure mode diagnosed and documented (short formulaic headers underperform in dense retrieval).

Meta · Data Science Intern (Quest Devices)

Product Analytics VR/Quest Experimentation SQL

Analyzed engagement for Quest experiences and peripherals (PCVR vs. native apps, Quest Pro vs. Quest 3/3S, locale-specific titles like JP-language apps, Virtual Desktop, Steam Link).

Hypotheses I tested

  • H1: PCVR bridge apps (e.g., Virtual Desktop / Steam Link) drive higher time-spent per MAU than comparable native titles because of content breadth.
  • H2: Quest 3/3S owners show higher retention after week 2 than Quest Pro owners due to broader game fit and price-to-value dynamics.
  • H3: Locale-specific apps exhibit distinct session-time distributions and store-funnel behavior vs. global averages.

Issues faced

  • Attribution across PCVR vs. native surfaces; session stitching and device/app identifiers.
  • Sparse / long-tail titles skewing central tendency; required robust summaries and winsorization.
  • Metric definition drift (time-spent, MAU/WAU); standardized to canonical product metrics.

How I grew

  • Designed decision-ready dashboards and wrote clear experiment readouts for PMs and leadership.
  • Tightened SQL chops for large-scale event data over 6M+ MAU; added guardrails for cohorting and retention cuts.
  • Improved stakeholder alignment (problem framing → metrics → caveats → next steps) under privacy constraints.

Responsible Data Science · Diabetes Fairness Audit

Fairlearn Bias Mitigation Model Audits

Audited demographic bias in a hospital readmission prediction model on the diabetes hospital dataset. Trained a baseline Random Forest, then implemented three families of fairness interventions and compared their accuracy-fairness tradeoffs.

What I did

  • Pre-processing: CorrelationRemover to reduce feature-sensitive attribute correlation.
  • In-processing: AdversarialFairnessClassifier across four alpha values.
  • Post-processing: ThresholdOptimizer with equalized odds constraints.
  • Ran each intervention across 10 random seeds, comparing demographic parity, equalized odds, selection rate, FPR/FNR differences, and overall performance.
